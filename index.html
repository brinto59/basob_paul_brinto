About

I recently graduated with a degree in Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE) from Rajshahi University of Engineering & Technology (RUET). My research experience includes working under the supervision of Dr. Mohammod Abdul Motin, where my work focused on developing advanced deep learning models and Transformer architectures for biomedical signal classification. My undergraduate thesis focused on Load Frequency Control in a Two-Area Power System under the supervision of Dr. Md. Sohel Rana. To date, my research has been published in AII 2024 (conference), and I currently have a manuscript under revision in THMS (journal). Additionally, I have industry experience working as a Data Scientist at Synnax Lab.
